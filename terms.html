2. What the app does

VinylSweep uses your camera to identify vinyl records, looks up their release details and marketplace pricing, and compares that to a price you enter so you can judge a purchase. It presents a recommendation such as Buy, Good Deal, Fair Price, or Skip.

3. Valuations are estimates

This section matters more than any other, so it is stated plainly.

  • Prices come from third-party marketplace data, principally Discogs. That data may be incomplete, stale, or wrong.
  • Record identification is performed by automated image recognition. It will sometimes identify the wrong release — pressings, reissues, and regional variants can differ enormously in value while looking nearly identical.
  • Condition dramatically affects value, and the app cannot assess the condition of a record from a photograph of its sleeve.
  • A recommendation is a convenience, not a professional opinion. It is not an appraisal, valuation, insurance estimate, or investment advice.

Every buying decision is yours. Verify anything that matters before you spend money on it, and do not rely on the app as the sole basis for a purchase, a sale, or an insurance claim.

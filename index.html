How it works

Three steps. A few seconds.

Scan the record.

Point your iPhone camera at the cover. It works without a barcode, and you do not have to type any names.

Type in the price.

Enter what the shop wants. VinylSweep compares it with the cheapest offer online for that same record.

Get the answer.

One word: Steal, Good Deal, Fair Price or Skip. The numbers are right below it. Then buy it or put it back.

Live Sweep

Scan a whole crate at once.

Flip through the records and tap once for each one. Up to 8 records per scan. You see what each one costs online.

Old copy or new copy?

Same album. Different price.

An original from 1978 is worth more than a reissue from 2019. VinylSweep works out which one you are holding.

Your collection

Keep what you find.

Save the records you own and the ones you are looking for. VinylSweep tells you when it spots one from your list, and adds up what your collection is worth.

Listen first

Hear it before you buy.

Play a short sample of a song, when one is available for the record.

What it costs

Free to try. Pro if you use it a lot.

VinylSweep is free to download, and your first 3 scans are free. Each scan checks up to 8 records. Scanning a barcode and searching by name stay free after that.

With VinylSweep Pro you can scan as much as you want: $3.99 a month, or $24.99 a year with the first week free. Prices vary by country.

Where the prices come from

The online price is the cheapest offer for that record on the Discogs marketplace right now, plus estimated shipping to your region. It is an estimate, not what the record last sold for. The app screens on this page show prices in euros; the app uses your own currency. The drawn covers and their prices are examples.
